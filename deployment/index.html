As a researcher, I've worked with a wide range of remote sensing datasets, including multispectral and hyperspectral imagery, as well as LiDAR point cloud, SAR, and thermal imagery. I intended to investigate integrating datasets from different kinds of remote sensing sensors for applications in a wide range of sectors, including forestry, agriculture, target detection and identification, and environmental monitoring. I sincerely appreciate natural landscapes, so I pursued a master's degree in remote sensing. In my spare time, I like exploring new places, photographing natural landscapes and live moments, and making new friends.
